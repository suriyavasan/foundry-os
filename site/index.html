You write down what you want. An AI Co-founder turns it into goals, hires AI specialists, hands out the work and tells you what happened. It runs on your own computer, with your own AI. You approve anything that costs real money.

Free and open source (MIT). No account with us. No tracking.

How it works

You set the direction. The company does the rest.

  1. Write a note

    Say what you want in plain words, like "Build a landing page for our first product." Every note gets an answer.

  2. The AI Co-founder plans

    It turns your note into goals and projects, picks a lead and replies with what it set up.

  3. HR hires, the team works

    If a skill is missing, HR finds or writes it and hires someone who has it. Specialists do the work and save files you can open.

  4. You stay in charge

    Work is reviewed. Anything with real money or real-world commitments waits for your yes.

What you get

A whole company, with the brakes built in.

An AI Co-founder

One agent whose whole job is managing the others. It triages your notes, sets goals and reports back.

HR that hires and trains

Skills are short instruction packs. HR scans them for safety, assigns them and hires the right role.

Command Center and Reflections

Type a note or a slash command and always get an answer. The Co-founder posts reflections, and the standards you endorse become company rules. (In progress.)

Guardrails in code

Risk tiers, approvals, budgets, rate limits and an emergency stop. Enforced by the program, not by asking nicely.

Runs on your machine

Your data sits in a folder on your computer. There is no Foundry cloud.

Bring your own AI

OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, any OpenAI-compatible service, or a free local model with Ollama. Free models are preferred by default.

Coding tools do the work

Optionally, employees work through Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode, while your AI key runs management. (In progress.)

Reach it from anywhere

Open it on your phone, through a secure tunnel, on your own domain, with a company email address.

Humans can join

Invite people. They sit in the same org chart and can be given tasks like any agent.

Install

Pick one. Then run foundryos.

These install options are available from the first release, v0.1.0. They are not published yet.

From v0.1.0

Homebrew

macOS and Linux. Updates with brew upgrade foundry-os.

brew install suriyavasan/tap/foundry-os
From v0.1.0

One-line installer

macOS and Linux, no Homebrew needed. Updates with foundryos update.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/suriyavasan/foundry-os/main/install.sh | sh
From v0.1.0

Docker

Any system, including Windows. Then open localhost:8700.

docker run -d --name foundry-os -p 8700:8700 -v foundry-os:/data ghcr.io/suriyavasan/foundry-os:latest

Want it today? You can run it from source. It needs a terminal and a few developer tools.

What we have and have not tested. It was run end to end on one Mac with a small local model. Docker, Linux, Windows, PostgreSQL and cloud AI models have not been tested yet. A small local model is slow and only partly autonomous. The full record is in the roadmap.

Safety

Autonomy with real limits.

The limits are code. An AI that is confused or tricked still cannot go past them.

No automated sign-ups

Agents never create accounts that need a CAPTCHA, a phone check or accepting terms. You connect each account once.

Real money needs you

Domains, subscriptions and anything over budget wait for your approval, tied to the exact amount.

Agents cannot raise their own limits

Permissions, budgets and rules can be changed only by people. There is also an emergency stop.

Secrets stay hidden

Keys are stored encrypted, are write-only on screen, and are never shown to agents or written to logs.

Honest numbers

Every cost is labelled verified, estimated or unknown. An estimate is never shown as a charge.

Known gaps are published

We list what is not covered, such as unencrypted backups, in the security notes.
