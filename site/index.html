[ The whole company ]

The whole company. No hiring slowdown.

Skip the job posts, the onboarding and the status meetings. Foundry gives you a working org on day one, with the brakes built in.

  • An AI Co-founder that triages your notes, sets goals and reports back
  • HR that hires and trains, with skills scanned for safety
  • Guardrails in code: risk tiers, approvals, budgets, an emergency stop
  • Your data, your machine. There is no Foundry cloud
  • Bring your own AI: OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or free local Ollama
  • Reach it from anywhere, on your phone or your own domain

[ How it works ]

Autonomous by default. Yours by design.

  1. 01

    Write a note

    Say what you want in plain words. Every note gets an answer.

  2. 02

    The Co-founder plans

    It turns your note into goals and projects, picks a lead and replies with what it set up.

  3. 03

    The team works

    HR hires the right roles. Specialists do the work and their output is reviewed.

  4. 04

    You stay in charge

    Anything with real money or real-world commitments waits for your yes.

[ Safety ]

Autonomy, with real limits.

The limits are code. An AI that is confused or tricked still cannot go past them.

$

Real money needs you

Purchases, subscriptions and anything over budget wait for your approval, tied to the exact amount.

⌘

No self-promotion

Agents can never change their own permissions, budgets or rules. Only people can.

■

Emergency stop

One button pauses the whole company, mid-task.

✱

Secrets stay hidden

Keys are encrypted, write-only on screen, and never shown to agents or written to logs.

✓

No automated sign-ups

Agents never create accounts that need a CAPTCHA, a phone check or accepting terms.

≈

Honest numbers

Every cost is labelled verified, estimated or unknown. An estimate is never shown as a charge.

[ Install ]

Pick one. Then run foundryos.

Each option installs the latest release. Every change that passes the tests is released automatically.

Homebrew

macOS and Linux. Update with brew upgrade foundry-os.

brew install suriyavasan/tap/foundry-os

One-line installer

macOS and Linux, no Homebrew. Update with foundryos update.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/suriyavasan/foundry-os/main/install.sh | sh

Docker

Any system, including Windows. Then open localhost:8700.

docker run -d --name foundry-os -p 8700:8700 -v foundry-os:/data ghcr.io/suriyavasan/foundry-os:latest

What we have and have not tested. It was run end to end on one Mac with a small local model. Docker, Linux, Windows, PostgreSQL and cloud AI models have not been tested yet. A small local model is slow and only partly autonomous. Prefer source? Run it from source.

[ FAQ ]

Frequently asked questions

What is Foundry AI OS?

An operating system for a company run by AI agents. You write notes; an AI Co-founder plans, HR hires AI specialists, the team does the work, and you approve anything that costs real money. It was formerly called "Company OS".

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything lives in a folder on your computer. There is no Foundry cloud and no account with us. Your AI provider sees what you send it.

How much does it cost?

Foundry is free. A local model through Ollama costs nothing but electricity. A cloud AI charges you per use, billed by that provider. Costs we show for paid AI are estimates, labelled as such.

Can agents spend my money?

Not beyond what you allow. Spending outside AI usage needs your approval by default, tied to the exact amount, and agents cannot change their own budgets or rules.

Do I need to be a programmer?

Not to use it. Installing today still means pasting one command into a terminal.

Does it really run a company by itself?

It runs the loop without you approving each step. With a small local model it got partway in our test. A stronger model should do better; we have not tested that yet. Treat it as something to watch and guide.
